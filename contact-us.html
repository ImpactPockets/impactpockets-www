FAQ'S

Before you send an inquiry, review the basics of our partnership and selection process.

What is Impact Pockets?

Impact Pockets helps experienced operators turn a focused investment opportunity into a structured private equity fund. Our team supports strategy, formation, fundraising preparation, and ongoing operations.

Who can apply to become a fund operator with Impact Pockets?

Tell us about your operating experience, target market, and fund concept. We review each inquiry to decide whether the opportunity and partnership are a strong mutual fit.

How does the fund incubation process work?

We begin with a conversation and business analysis. Qualified opportunities move through strategy, structure, vendor selection, investor preparation, and formal fund formation.

What industries do you focus on?

Our focus follows the experience of the operator and the quality of the opportunity. Include your industry and investment thesis in the form so we can evaluate it in context.
